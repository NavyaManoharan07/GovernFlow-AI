# Business Registration Requirements

> **MOCK / DEMONSTRATION DATA — not real current law.** All statute names, section numbers, and thresholds below are invented for the GovFlow AI hackathon demo and must never be treated as legal advice or a real government source.

## Overview

Any person operating a for-profit business within Demo Municipality must register the business entity before conducting commercial activity, opening a business bank account, or applying for any dependent license (tax registration, food license, local approval).

## Numbered Requirements

- REQ-BR-1: The applicant must register the business with the Demo Registrar of Companies before commencing operations. (Service: business_registration) (Source: Demo Municipal Business Code §4.2 (MOCK))
- REQ-BR-2: The applicant must select a business structure (sole proprietorship, partnership, or private limited company) at the time of registration. (Service: business_registration) (Source: Demo Municipal Business Code §4.3 (MOCK))
- REQ-BR-3: A registered business name must not duplicate an existing active registration within Demo Municipality. (Service: business_registration) (Source: Demo Municipal Business Code §4.5 (MOCK))
- REQ-BR-4: The applicant must provide a valid local business address (registered office) within Demo Municipality's jurisdiction. (Service: business_registration) (Source: Demo Municipal Business Code §4.6 (MOCK))
- REQ-BR-5: The applicant must be at least 18 years of age and hold a valid government-issued identity document. (Service: business_registration) (Source: Demo Municipal Business Code §4.8 (MOCK))
- REQ-BR-6: Registration fees are due at submission and vary by business structure; a private limited company incurs the highest tier fee. (Service: business_registration) (Source: Demo Municipal Business Code §4.11 (MOCK))
- REQ-BR-7: Business registration must be renewed annually within 30 days of the registration anniversary date. (Service: business_registration) (Source: Demo Municipal Business Code §4.14 (MOCK))

## Processing

Registration is typically reviewed within 3-5 business days in the mock demo timeline. Once approved, the applicant receives a Business Registration Number, which is a prerequisite for tax registration, food license, and local approval applications.
