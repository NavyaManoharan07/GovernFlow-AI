# Food License Requirements

> **MOCK / DEMONSTRATION DATA — not real current law.** All statute names, section numbers, and thresholds below are invented for the GovFlow AI hackathon demo and must never be treated as legal advice or a real government source.

## Overview

Any business that processes, packages, stores, or sells food products within Demo Municipality must hold a valid Food License issued by the Demo Food Safety Authority before commencing food-related operations.

## Numbered Requirements

- REQ-FL-1: The applicant must hold an approved Business Registration Number before applying for a food license. (Service: food_license) (Source: Demo State Food Safety Act §3.1 (MOCK))
- REQ-FL-2: The applicant must designate a Food Safety Supervisor who has completed a recognized food-handling safety course. (Service: food_license) (Source: Demo State Food Safety Act §3.3 (MOCK))
- REQ-FL-3: The proposed food-processing premises must pass a hygiene and facility inspection prior to license approval. (Service: food_license) (Source: Demo State Food Safety Act §3.5 (MOCK))
- REQ-FL-4: Small food-processing businesses (fewer than 10 employees and under 500,000 Demo Currency Units annual turnover) qualify for the simplified Small Food Business license track with reduced fees. (Service: food_license) (Source: Demo State Food Safety Act §3.7 (MOCK))
- REQ-FL-5: The applicant must submit a product list describing every food category to be processed or sold (e.g. packaged snacks, dairy, baked goods). (Service: food_license) (Source: Demo State Food Safety Act §3.8 (MOCK))
- REQ-FL-6: A valid water quality test certificate for the processing premises, dated within the last 12 months, must be submitted. (Service: food_license) (Source: Demo State Food Safety Act §3.10 (MOCK))
- REQ-FL-7: The Food License must be renewed annually and is subject to unannounced hygiene re-inspection. (Service: food_license) (Source: Demo State Food Safety Act §3.15 (MOCK))

## Processing

Food license review in the mock demo timeline includes a simulated facility inspection step and is typically approved within 5-7 business days once all required documents are validated.
