# Tax Registration Requirements

> **MOCK / DEMONSTRATION DATA — not real current law.** All statute names, section numbers, and thresholds below are invented for the GovFlow AI hackathon demo and must never be treated as legal advice or a real government source.

## Overview

Every registered business operating within Demo Municipality must obtain a Tax Registration Number (TRN) from the Demo Tax Department before issuing invoices or collecting sales tax.

## Numbered Requirements

- REQ-TX-1: The applicant must hold an approved Business Registration Number before applying for tax registration. (Service: tax_registration) (Source: Demo Revenue Code §7.1 (MOCK))
- REQ-TX-2: The applicant must declare an estimated annual turnover figure at the time of application. (Service: tax_registration) (Source: Demo Revenue Code §7.2 (MOCK))
- REQ-TX-3: Businesses with estimated annual turnover above 4,000,000 Demo Currency Units must register for the standard tax tier; businesses below that threshold may register for the simplified small-business tax tier. (Service: tax_registration) (Source: Demo Revenue Code §7.4 (MOCK))
- REQ-TX-4: The applicant must provide a valid bank account in the registered business's name for tax remittance purposes. (Service: tax_registration) (Source: Demo Revenue Code §7.6 (MOCK))
- REQ-TX-5: Food-processing businesses must additionally register for the Demo Food Products Excise category alongside standard tax registration. (Service: tax_registration) (Source: Demo Revenue Code §7.9 (MOCK))
- REQ-TX-6: Tax registration must be renewed annually and is automatically suspended if business registration lapses. (Service: tax_registration) (Source: Demo Revenue Code §7.12 (MOCK))

## Processing

Tax registration in the mock demo timeline is typically approved within 1-2 business days once the Business Registration Number is verified.
